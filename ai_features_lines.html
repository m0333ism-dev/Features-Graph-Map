    dep_cop_ratio is constant in this corpus and is displayed at zero. Features remain in their supplied order; connecting them does not imply a temporal or causal sequence.

    What these graphs can tell you

    These are descriptive views of the original 5,046-document corpus. Normalization uses the same full-corpus minimum and maximum for each feature in both classes, even after a genre filter. It was not fitted on a held-out training split and is not a deployable scoring transform.

    Median lines and middle-50% bands summarize each feature separately. They do not preserve joint feature interactions. Use the rebuilt interaction explorer for document tuples, 3D views, configurations, reconstructions and genre exceptions.

    The RAID explorer is a separate study with its own discovery, development and frozen evaluation. Its results should not be attributed to these original-corpus line plots.
